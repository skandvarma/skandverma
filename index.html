Research

Generative Artificial Intelligence and Its Application in Metallurgical Engineering: Current Situation and Future Perspectives

Himeshkumar Patel & Skand Verma · Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR), 2026 · Accepted, in press

A review of generative AI for the extractive industry. It covers five generative model families and their uses in pyrometallurgy, comminution, flotation, hydrometallurgy, ore sorting, plant digitalization, plant reliability and inverse materials design. It also separates confirmed generative-AI studies from discriminative-ML and digital-twin work. Generative AI is most useful where metallurgical data are sparse, noisy, high-dimensional or framed as inverse design. Traditional ML remains the better choice for many well-labelled tabular tasks.
